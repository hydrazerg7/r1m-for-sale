What the R1M comes with from the factory

The R1M is the limited, reservation‑only version of the R1. Everything below is standard equipment on this bike.

Engine & drivetrain

  • 998 cc crossplane‑crank (CP4) inline‑four, liquid‑cooled, DOHC 16‑valve
  • Titanium fracture‑split connecting rods (first on any production motorcycle)
  • Roughly 200 hp at the crank with ram air, 14,000 rpm redline
  • Six‑speed gearbox with assist‑and‑slipper clutch
  • Factory quickshifter (QS) for clutchless upshifts
  • 4.5 gal fuel tank

Electronics

  • Six‑axis Inertial Measurement Unit feeding every rider aid
  • Lean‑sensitive traction, slide, wheelie and launch control (TCS, SCS, LIF, LCS)
  • Four rider‑programmable ride modes and four power maps
  • ABS with Unified Brake System (UBS)
  • Full‑color TFT dash with Street and Track layouts and lap timer
  • LED headlights, position lights and tail light

R1M‑only equipment

  • Öhlins Electronic Racing Suspension (ERS): semi‑active 43 mm fork and TTX36 shock, three automatic and three manual modes, set from the dash
  • Communication Control Unit (CCU): built‑in GPS datalogger, Wi‑Fi to the Y‑TRAC app for lap analysis and setting changes
  • Carbon fiber front fairing, side fairings, front fender and tail section
  • Brushed aluminum fuel tank cover and polished aluminum swingarm
  • Wider 200/55 ZR17 rear tire on a 6.0 in rim (standard R1 runs a 190)
  • Silver Blu Carbon livery, sold by online reservation only

Chassis & brakes

  • Aluminum Deltabox frame with magnesium rear subframe
  • Cast magnesium 10‑spoke wheels
  • Dual 320 mm front discs with four‑piston monoblock calipers, 220 mm rear
  • Wet weight about 443 lb; seat height about 33.9 in; wheelbase 55.3 in
